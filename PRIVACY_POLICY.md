# Privacy Policy — Aura

**Last Updated:** August 21, 2026

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains what information the Aura Discord bot ("Aura", "the Bot", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that data. By using Aura, you agree to the practices described here.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide its features, Aura may store:
- **Discord identifiers** — your Discord User ID and the Server (Guild) ID of servers Aura is used in, used to associate settings, playlists, and preferences with the correct user or server.
- **Music data** — playlists and liked songs you save, including track titles, URLs/URIs, and source platform.
- **Server configuration** — settings server admins configure, such as custom prefix, ignored channels, and auto-react/auto-respond triggers.
- **Usage status** — premium status, admin/owner status, and moderation status (e.g. blacklist), where applicable.
- **Voice channel ID** — temporarily, only while actively playing music in a voice channel, to maintain playback.

Aura does **not** intentionally collect or store the content of your regular Discord messages, passwords, payment card details, or any information beyond what is described above.

## 3. How We Use Information
Collected data is used only to:
- Operate core features (music playback, playlists, liked songs, queue management).
- Apply per-server configuration and preferences.
- Enforce our [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) (e.g. blacklist enforcement).
- Improve reliability and troubleshoot issues.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Aura interacts with the following third parties as part of its normal operation:
- **Discord** — for bot connectivity; Discord's own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) governs data handled at the platform level.
- **Music platforms** (YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Deezer, JioSaavn) — to search for and retrieve track metadata/audio. Your search queries are sent to these platforms to fulfil playback requests.
- **Lavalink audio node(s)** — used to process and stream audio into voice channels. Depending on hosting configuration, this may be a self-hosted node or a third-party-operated node.

Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

## 5. Data Storage & Retention
Data described in Section 2 is stored in Aura's database for as long as needed to provide the related feature — for example, a playlist is stored until you delete it or request its removal. We take reasonable measures to protect stored data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Rights & Choices
You may:
- Delete your own playlists or liked songs at any time using Aura's commands.
- Request full deletion of your stored data by contacting us at [RasaVedic@Gmail.Com](mailto:RasaVedic@Gmail.Com).
- Ask a server administrator to remove server-level configuration data.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Aura is intended for use in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service, which requires users to meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from users who do not meet this requirement.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of Aura after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
